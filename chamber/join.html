NP Membership Benefits

Designed for nonprofit organizations advancing the Plainside Valley community.

  • Chamber directory listing
  • Access to nonprofit networking meetups
  • Community resource and training announcements
  • Invitations to selected member events

$120 per year

Bronze Membership Benefits

A practical starting point for local businesses ready to make new connections.

  • All NP-level directory and event benefits
  • Member business directory listing with website link
  • Discounted admission to chamber events
  • Quarterly small-business training sessions

$240 per year

Silver Membership Benefits

More ways to promote your organization and build your skills.

  • All Bronze-level benefits
  • One featured member social post each year
  • Two complimentary event admissions per year
  • Enhanced directory listing and event discounts

$450 per year

Gold Membership Benefits

Premium visibility and access for organizations ready to lead.

  • All Silver-level benefits
  • Rotating spotlight placement on the chamber home page
  • Priority sponsorship opportunities at chamber events
  • Four complimentary event admissions per year
  • One annual promotional workshop consultation
