Samoa Travel Guide Website Plan

Site Name

Samoa Travel Guide

This name was selected because it clearly communicates that the website provides travel-related information about Samoa.

Site Purpose

The website provides information about Samoa's tourist attractions, accommodations, events, transportation, and travel tips to help visitors plan memorable trips.

Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in Samoa during a family vacation?
  • Where can I find hotels and transportation information?
  • What cultural activities are available for visitors?
  • How can I contact local tourism services?

Color Scheme

Ocean Blue (#0077B6) - Headings, navigation, footer, buttons.

Sand Beige (#F4E1C1) - Background color and content areas.

Palm Green (#2D6A4F) - Highlights and accent elements.

Typography

Poppins - Headings and navigation.

Roboto - Body text and paragraphs.

Open Sans - Footer and supporting text.
