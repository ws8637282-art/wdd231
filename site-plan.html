WDD 231 · Project planning

Samoa Island Guide

A welcoming starting point for discovering the islands, culture, and practical details of Samoa.

01 · Identity

Site Name

Samoa Island Guide

The name clearly identifies the destination and promises useful guidance to travelers and curious readers. “Island Guide” is broad enough to cover places, culture, and trip-planning information as the project grows.

Optional domain idea: samoa-island-guide.org (example only; availability has not been checked)

02 · Audience and scope

Site Purpose

Samoa Island Guide will introduce visitors to Samoa and help them plan an informed, respectful trip. The site will feature a country overview, highlights and places to visit, cultural context, travel tips, and useful links for finding additional official information.

Its home page will orient first-time visitors with a concise introduction and clear paths to destination details and practical planning resources.

03 · Visitor questions

Scenarios

These questions represent common needs of people researching Samoa before or during a visit.

  • What are some memorable places and experiences to include in a first trip to Samoa?
  • What local customs should I understand so I can be a respectful visitor?
  • What practical information should I check before traveling to Samoa?

04 · Visual direction

Color Scheme

The palette draws on Samoa's ocean setting and warm island landscape. The colors below account for the colors used throughout this plan document, including its text, surfaces, links, and dividers.

  • Ocean navy · #123C4A
    Primary headings, header background, and footer.
  • Lagoon teal · #167C80
    Links, section markers, and interactive accents.
  • Coral · #B84F3A
    Small highlights, focus outlines, and key emphasis.
  • Warm sand · #F4EBDD
    Page background and soft panel surfaces.
  • Deep ink · #243238
    Body copy for readable contrast on light backgrounds.
  • Paper · #FFFAF3
    Light card surfaces and text on dark backgrounds.
  • Divider · #D7CDBF
    Subtle borders around panels and swatches.

05 · Reading and hierarchy

Typography

Playfair Display
Used for the page title and section headings to give the destination guide a distinctive editorial feel.
DM Sans
Used for paragraphs, lists, labels, and navigation because its clean shapes remain legible at small screen sizes.

06 · Home page structure

Wireframe

The home page should adapt from a single-column mobile layout to a wider desktop layout. The sketches for this assignment must be drawn by the student in black and white; an AI-generated HTML wireframe is not used here.

Sketch these two views by hand

  • Small viewport: stacked brand and menu button; page title and short introduction; featured destination image; vertically stacked overview and travel-information sections; simple footer.
  • Large viewport (desktop/tablet): brand and horizontal navigation; wider featured introduction; destination highlights arranged in columns; planning information and footer organized into clear multi-column regions.

Insert or attach your own photographed/scanned small- and large-viewport sketches when ready.
